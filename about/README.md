## The short version

Every piece of software I leaned on was good — genuinely good — and each one wanted its own subscription for the half I
actually used. I was paying five companies monthly to hold one week of my life in five places, and losing focus every
time I switched between them.

So this is my own roof instead. One workspace for capture, planning, focus and review, built on my habits rather than a
product manager's roadmap, and free in the plain sense: no account, no tiers, and a workspace that lives in my browser
as a file I own. The name is the point — a *motif* is a figure that repeats, which is what a habit is, and the whole
thing is a blend of the tools I love rather than a copy of any one of them.

If you found this repository by accident, the [README](../README.md) is the product; this folder is the reason.
